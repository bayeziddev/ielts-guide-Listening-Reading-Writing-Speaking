# IELTS Speaking Module Overview

The **IELTS Speaking** test is a face-to-face interview with a certified examiner. It is designed to assess your spoken English fluency, pronunciation, and ability to communicate effectively in a natural conversation.

## Test Format

The Speaking test is the same for both Academic and General Training candidates and lasts between 11 and 14 minutes.

| Part | Description | Duration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Part 1** | Introduction and general questions on familiar topics | 4-5 Minutes |
| **Part 2** | Individual long turn (speaking about a specific topic on a cue card) | 3-4 Minutes |
| **Part 3** | Two-way discussion on more abstract issues related to Part 2 | 4-5 Minutes |

## Success Factors

To achieve a high band score, you should focus on the following areas:
*   **Fluency and Coherence:** Speaking at length without undue hesitation.
*   **Lexical Resource:** Using a wide range of vocabulary and idiomatic expressions.
*   **Grammatical Range:** Employing complex sentence structures accurately.
*   **Pronunciation:** Being easy to understand, even with an accent.
