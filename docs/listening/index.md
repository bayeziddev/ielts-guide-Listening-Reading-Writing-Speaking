# IELTS Listening Module Overview

The **IELTS Listening** test is designed to assess your ability to understand spoken English in a variety of contexts, ranging from everyday social interactions to academic lectures. The test is the same for both Academic and General Training candidates.

## Test Structure

The Listening test consists of four sections, each increasing in difficulty. You will listen to four recordings and answer 40 questions in total.

| Section | Context | Number of Speakers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Section 1** | Everyday social context (e.g., booking a hotel) | Two speakers |
| **Section 2** | Everyday social context (e.g., a speech about local facilities) | One speaker |
| **Section 3** | Educational or training context (e.g., a tutor and student) | Up to four speakers |
| **Section 4** | Academic subject (e.g., a university lecture) | One speaker |

## Key Skills Tested

To succeed in the Listening module, you must develop several core competencies:

*   **Listening for Gist:** Understanding the main idea of a conversation or talk.
*   **Listening for Specific Information:** Identifying dates, names, or numbers.
*   **Understanding Opinions:** Recognizing the speaker's attitude or purpose.
*   **Following an Argument:** Keeping track of complex ideas in academic discussions.
