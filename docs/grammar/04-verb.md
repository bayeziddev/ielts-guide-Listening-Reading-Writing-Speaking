# 4. Verb

> **Chapter focus:** verb types, auxiliaries, modals, agreement, and active/passive voice.

## How to study this lesson

Read the explanation first, then say the examples aloud. Change one detail in each example, such as the subject, time, or place. Complete the practice without looking back and use the answer key to understand the reason for each answer. Finish by writing two sentences about your own life or city.

A **verb** tells us what happens or what state exists. Every complete English clause needs a finite verb. Verbs carry information about tense, agreement, voice, and possibility.

### Verb types

An **action verb** describes an activity: *measure, reduce, construct*. A **stative verb** describes a state: *know, believe, seem, belong*. Stative verbs are not normally used in the continuous form: **I understand the problem**, not usually **I am understanding the problem**.

A **transitive verb** takes an object: **The policy reduced emissions**. An **intransitive verb** does not: **The population grew**. Some verbs can be both: **The company increased prices** and **Prices increased**.

**Auxiliary verbs** help form questions, negatives, tenses, and passive structures. The primary auxiliaries are *be, have,* and *do*. Modal verbs include *can, could, may, might, must, should, will,* and *would*. A modal is followed by the base form: **Governments should invest**, not **should invests** or **should to invest**.

### Agreement and verb patterns

The verb must agree with its subject: **The result shows** but **The results show**. In a phrase such as **The number of students is increasing**, the subject is *number*, not *students*. After *make, let,* and most modals, use the base form: **The policy made people change** and **can improve**. After a preposition, use a noun or *-ing* form: **by reducing waste**, not **by reduce waste**.

### Active and passive voice

In the active voice, the subject performs the action: **Researchers collected the data**. In the passive voice, the receiver is the focus: **The data were collected by researchers**. Use the passive when the process or result matters more than the actor, especially in Academic Task 1 process descriptions.

### Practice

Correct the verbs: (a) The results indicates a clear trend. (b) Governments should to improve public transport. (c) The data was collected from three schools. (d) This method allows students improving accuracy.

**Answers:** (a) **indicate**, (b) **should improve**, (c) **The data were collected** is the formal academic choice; *data was* is common in less formal usage when *data* means a mass of information, (d) **allows students to improve**.
