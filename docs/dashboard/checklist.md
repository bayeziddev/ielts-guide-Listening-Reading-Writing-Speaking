# Preparation Checklist

> **Chapter focus:** A complete study path.

## Start here

A strong IELTS plan has five stages: understand the test, build language foundations, practise question types, complete timed tests, and review errors. Do not jump directly to full tests if the task format is still confusing.

## What you need to know

Prepare identification and test-day materials according to your centre instructions. Prepare study materials such as official sample tests, a notebook, a timer, headphones, and a quiet speaking-recording space. Your plan should include Listening, Reading, Writing, Speaking, vocabulary, grammar, and weekly review.

## Step-by-step plan

1. Confirm your test purpose, version, and date.
2. Take a realistic baseline task.
3. Record evidence instead of guessing your level.
4. Practise one skill with a clear time limit.
5. Review errors and repeat the same skill later.

## Practical method

Use a weekly cycle: learn one skill, practise it untimed, practise it timed, review mistakes, and retest. Keep one rest period so study remains sustainable.

## Guided practice

Create a seven-day plan with one measurable task for each day and one full review day.

## Self-check

- Can I explain the main idea of this page to another learner?
- What evidence shows my current strength?
- What single action will I complete this week?
- What mistake will I check before my next test?
