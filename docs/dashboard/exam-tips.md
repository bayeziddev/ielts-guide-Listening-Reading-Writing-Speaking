# Exam Day Tips

> **Chapter focus:** Stay calm and follow instructions.

## Start here

Exam performance depends on preparation and careful procedure. Arrive early, bring the required identification, and follow the centre’s current instructions. Check official communication because local procedures can change.

## What you need to know

During Listening, follow the recording and do not let one missed answer destroy the next one. During Reading, manage time and answer every question. During Writing, plan, develop, and check. During Speaking, answer naturally and ask for clarification only when you genuinely need it.

## Step-by-step plan

1. Confirm your test purpose, version, and date.
2. Take a realistic baseline task.
3. Record evidence instead of guessing your level.
4. Practise one skill with a clear time limit.
5. Review errors and repeat the same skill later.

## Practical method

Sleep normally, eat familiar food, arrive with time to spare, and avoid learning a large list of new words on the morning of the test. Use breathing and a short mental reset when anxiety rises.

## Guided practice

Write a personal exam-morning routine and a recovery plan for three problems: missing one Listening answer, forgetting a word in Speaking, and finding a difficult Writing topic.

## Self-check

- Can I explain the main idea of this page to another learner?
- What evidence shows my current strength?
- What single action will I complete this week?
- What mistake will I check before my next test?
