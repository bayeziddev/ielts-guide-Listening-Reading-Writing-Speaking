# Band Score Scale

> **Chapter focus:** Understand scores before setting a target.

## Start here

IELTS reports a band from 0 to 9. Listening and Reading scores are based on correct answers, while Writing and Speaking are judged with public criteria. A target is useful only when you know which skill needs improvement.

> **বাংলা সহায়তা:** পরীক্ষার নাম বা band মুখস্থ করলেই প্রস্তুতি সম্পূর্ণ হয় না। নিজের বর্তমান অবস্থান মাপুন, দুর্বল জায়গা চিহ্নিত করুন, এবং ছোট ছোট নিয়মিত কাজ করুন।

## What you need to know

Use official descriptors as a learning map, not as a promise. Band 6 usually means the learner can communicate adequately but still has noticeable errors. Band 7 requires more flexible language and better control. The exact conversion from raw answers can vary by test version, so use official practice materials.

## Step-by-step plan

1. Confirm your test purpose, version, and date.
2. Take a realistic baseline task.
3. Record evidence instead of guessing your level.
4. Practise one skill with a clear time limit.
5. Review errors and repeat the same skill later.

## Practical method

Make a baseline test, record each skill, identify the weakest criterion, and repeat after two weeks. Never compare one raw score from Listening with a Writing band as if they were the same measurement.

## Guided practice

Write your target band, current evidence, weakest skill, weekly practice hours, and review date.

## Self-check

- Can I explain the main idea of this page to another learner?
- What evidence shows my current strength?
- What single action will I complete this week?
- What mistake will I check before my next test?
