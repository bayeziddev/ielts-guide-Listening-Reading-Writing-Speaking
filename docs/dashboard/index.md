# IELTS Preparation Dashboard

Welcome to your central hub for IELTS preparation. This dashboard provides a high-level overview of the test, scoring systems, and essential checklists to ensure you are fully prepared for exam day.

## IELTS at a Glance

The International English Language Testing System (IELTS) is the world's most popular English language proficiency test for higher education and global migration.

| Feature | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Total Duration** | 2 Hours and 45 Minutes |
| **Versions** | Academic and General Training |
| **Score Range** | Band 1.0 (Non-user) to Band 9.0 (Expert user) |
| **Validity** | Typically 2 years |
