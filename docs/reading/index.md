# IELTS Reading Module Overview

The **IELTS Reading** test evaluates your ability to read and understand complex texts, identify main ideas, and extract specific details. While the format is similar for both Academic and General Training, the types of texts differ significantly.

## Academic vs. General Training

The Reading module is one of the two parts of the test that varies depending on the version you are taking:

| Feature | Academic Reading | General Training Reading |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Source** | Books, journals, magazines, and newspapers | Notices, advertisements, handbooks, and documents |
| **Complexity** | High (Academic/Professional) | Moderate (Everyday/Workplace) |
| **Number of Texts** | Three long texts | Multiple short to medium texts |

## Essential Reading Techniques

Success in this module requires more than just reading; you must master specific techniques to manage the strict time limit:

*   **Skimming:** Reading quickly to get a general overview of the content.
*   **Scanning:** Looking for specific words, numbers, or facts.
*   **Close Reading:** Analyzing specific sentences to understand complex arguments.
